# Delivery record and post-parity roadmap

The exhaustive, scan-derived implementation contract is
[`original-us-parity-checklist.md`](original-us-parity-checklist.md). That
checklist, not this summary, is the acceptance authority.

## Completed: playable physical rules

- Original 26 by 19 board topology, doors, reveal state, occupancy, stairs,
  room/corridor searches, and center-to-center visibility
- Four-Hero turn order, all movement/action orderings, combat, defense, death,
  traps, treasure, elemental spells, Chaos spells, equipment, artifacts,
  between-quest Armory, and campaign persistence
- Computer Zargon movement, pathfinding, target selection, attacks, spell
  policy, summoned figures, and hidden-information reveal behavior
- Visible Earth-scaled rigid-body dice for movement, attacks, defense, saves,
  healing, trap rolls, Wandering Monster attacks, and quest-specific effects

## Completed: original-US campaign

- Edition profile and exact source provenance for the North American/US rules,
  cards, Armory, components, and Quest Book
- All 14 Game System maps and every printed placement, note, objective, reward,
  and exceptional rule represented as typed data and executable logic
- Seeded rule/plot replay index, full disk-data conformance checks, and an
  engine-produced beginning/end campaign oracle for all 14 chapters
- Fresh-campaign flow from the 3D box through quest selection, Hero setup,
  elemental spell division, play, Armory visits, and the Champion reward

## Completed: physical presentation and interaction

- Calibrated board scan, box, Information Screen, Quest Book, Character Cards,
  Character Sheets, Armory, action cards, public Monster cards, private Zargon
  stacks, spell hands, and discard piles
- Required real GLBs for every classic figure, furniture item, door, marker,
  removable dressing piece, and die; required model slots fail fast when absent
- Four Hero stations, Zargon station, enlarged oak table, Gothic great-hall
  backdrop/set, animated flames, figure hops, combat lunges, recoil, and defeat
- Camera-followed actions, camera-relative movement, clickable A* destinations,
  pinch/wheel zoom, context-sensitive ornamented OSD, and clickable action
  buttons with bounded responsive layouts

## Optional post-parity enhancements

These are product extensions rather than missing original-US rules:

- Mid-quest save/resume and an optional confirm/undo accessibility layer
- Selectable faithful, stronger, or cinematic Zargon personalities
- Additional accessibility settings, localization, and controller remapping
- Higher-detail licensed room geometry or relit photogrammetry when a clean
  source asset becomes available
- Broader seeded simulation reports for AI strength and campaign balance
