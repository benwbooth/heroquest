# Local art pack

The renderer can combine an authentic board scan with transparent photographs
or scan cutouts of every base-game figure and furniture type. These files are
private inputs and remain ignored by Git.

The default root is `assets/local/editions/original-us/` when that directory is
installed, falling back to `assets/local/` for a hand-built pack. Set
`HEROQUEST_ART_DIR` to use another folder. Run `tools/audit-local-art.sh` to see
which slots are active.

## Board

Use one of `board-scan.png`, `board-scan.jpg`, or `board-scan.jpeg`. Crop it to
the outside edge of the playable board, viewed directly from above, with the
top board row at the top of the image.

If the image retains a printed border or logo panel, add a sibling
`board-calibration.json` containing the pixel bounds of the 26 by 19 playable
grid. The renderer keeps the full scan visible while aligning pieces to those
bounds.

## Figure cutouts

Put tightly cropped, transparent PNGs under `figures/`:

- `barbarian.png`, `dwarf.png`, `elf.png`, and `wizard.png`
- `goblin.png`, `orc.png`, `fimir.png`, `skeleton.png`, `zombie.png`, and
  `mummy.png`
- `chaos-warrior.png`, `gargoyle.png`, and `chaos-sorcerer.png`

The 2021 filenames `abomination.png`, `dread-warrior.png`, and
`dread-sorcerer.png` are accepted as fallbacks for the corresponding original
US pieces. Art-backed figures retain a 3D colored base and turn to face the
camera. A missing type retains its procedural 3D prototype.

## Furniture cutouts

Put transparent PNGs under `props/`: `table.png`, `chest.png`,
`bookcase.png`, `throne.png`, and `weapon-rack.png`.

The stairway is a curved 2x2 relief GLB based on the Quest Book symbol. Do not
use the four-up tile-sheet crop as `stairs.png`; those are secret-door tiles.

For every cutout, leave transparent padding only where it is part of the
silhouette. Front-on photographs give the most physical result. The current
runtime displays the board, figures, furniture, four Character Cards and
Character Sheets, elemental spell hands, the Armory, Zargon's decks, all eight
Monster cards, and the selected Quest Book page. Card clicks, sheet editing,
and deck state still belong to the rules/UI milestones.

## Original US scan-pack layout

Keep master PDFs under `scans/`, the downloaded source archive under `source/`,
and renderer/UI derivatives in these directories:

- `board-scan.jpg`
- `card-sheets/`, `tile-sheets/`, `quest-pages/`, and `rulebook-pages/`
- `box-pages/`, `character-sheet/`, `armory-pages/`, `poster-pages/`, and
  `extras-pages/`
- `tabletop/`, generated by `tools/extract-original-us-tabletop-art.sh` as a
  compact runtime cache of crops from those master scans
- `components/`, generated by `tools/extract-original-us-components.sh`; these
  are the exact cardboard faces layered over the door, furniture, trap,
  blocked-square, secret-door, and skull GLBs

These scans do not include standalone photographs or meshes of the miniatures,
furniture, dice, rats, or skull fittings. Their separately audited GLBs live in
`models/`; the tiny removable fittings are reproducibly reconstructed by
`tools/build-project-models.py` from original-component photographic reference.
